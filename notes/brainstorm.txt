Modes:
  Planning - build a workout plan
  Active - execute a workout, often according to preexisting workout plan
  Record - record a workout
  Review - observe progress through lists and graphs

Planning Actions
  Add Exercise
  Make workout (exercises, RPE, goal weight/reps for each exercise)
  Make Mesocycle
  Make Macrocycle
  

Active Actions
  Start workout
    Specify the associated planned workout
  Record sets & weight for each workout


Add workout
  Exercise: Select(name) weight #reps button[remove] button[duplicate]


Workout Plan Set Format
   ExerciseName: x reps of y weight for z sets
   ExerciseName: x reps of y_1, y_2, y_3,... weight
  ExerciseName: x_1,x_2,x_3,... of y weight
  ExerciseName: x_1 reps of y_1 weight, x_2 reps of y_2 weight
